# The laptop dilemma: HP Spectre (a while ago) to Mac

Slug idea: `hp-spectre-to-macbook`

## Notes (Mikul's words)

I needed a laptop for high school (freshman year, 2019). Here were my requirements:
- slim and portable (prob 13 / 15")
- good display + touch screen
- I was big on using the iPad so I wanted a 2-in-1 with an optional stylus. It may seem silly, but at the time I exclusively used an iPad, so I assumed a laptop could be the same.
- had a GPU. I needed something performant since I did ML things.

I spent months researching laptops (remember, pre-AI). Surprisingly it was almost impossible to find something that fits the requirements at a reasonable price. There were always some sort of tradeoffs.

At the end I narrowed it to a ThinkPad (no GPU), Dell XPS 15 (not 2-in-1), HP Spectre x360 (hit all of them).

After a lot of debating, ended up going with the HP.

But that was not without its problems. I had to warranty it like 3 times due to something failing with it. Not to mention I was becoming fed up with it.

I forgot what the original issue was, but overheating was one of them. The second one I got I was happy with since it came with an OLED screen and a spec bump. But that failed too. Then the 3rd one also failed. Honestly I don't remember what broke on the second and third one, just that they did. No clue if I still have it, but that was what I thought was ideal.

I used it for my first year at college. Then that summer (2024) I became fed up. Battery, heating, Windows has its own set of problems.

At that point it was time.

Mac.

Spent a lot of time debating and researching here and ended up going with the MacBook Pro, M3 Pro, 18GB, 1TB storage. It was pricey but ran with it.

Now I'm fully into the Apple ecosystem. There is no turning back. Was one of the best decisions I made. Solved all my concerns and even better for engineering (I used Parallels for Windows when I needed it). Mac sometimes has its issues, but it's a lot less frequent.

Super happy now. I'm not going back to Windows.
