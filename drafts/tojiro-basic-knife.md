# Tojiro Basic knife

Slug idea: `tojiro-basic-knife`

## Notes (Mikul's words)

After I started cooking, I wanted to get myself a good-quality knife. Started talking to cooking friends and doing research online. Original budget was around $50.

Consistently saw the 2 chef knives (western) everywhere, the Victorinox Fibrox Pro and the Mercer Genesis. Was gonna buy one of em since several reviews said they were the best value for money.

From others I talked to, they said spending more is better, but not too much before there are diminishing returns. Was recommended some Japanese knives, including the Tojiro knife.

Went to the Korin store and tested their cheaper ones (their house brand, Togiharu). They charge more for the same knife as Tojiro because of their QA process.

Ended up getting the Tojiro Basic for $75 with shipping. It has the bang for buck with VG-10 steel and a really nice handle that shows it's a good knife. I use it a lot and it's a very good purchase.

It is a harder steel so it keeps its edge, but it's gonna be harder to sharpen later.

Went with the Basic since there is no real benefit with the regular (the DP line). Same steel, nicer finish, a lot more money.

Other reviews also say it is the best value for money (Japanese) knife, but I would say it is also a good value knife in general.
